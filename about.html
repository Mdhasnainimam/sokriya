Our story

We started in a garage with a soldering iron.

SkoriyaTech was founded by a handful of engineers who were frustrated with the artificial gap between "the people who design products" and "the people who manufacture them." We collapsed the gap.

Today we operate as a single integrated studio: research feeds engineering, engineering feeds manufacturing, and manufacturing feeds back into research. The result is faster cycles, fewer hand-offs, and products that survive the field — not just the lab.

We work with founders, Fortune 500s, defense primes, climate startups and everything in between. Our only requirement: you must want to build something that runs deep.
